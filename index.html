    <section class="about" id="about">

        <p>I am a professor in the Astronomy Department at the <a href="https://www.umass.edu/astronomy/" target="_blank">University of Massachusetts in Amherst</a>. I received my Ph.D. from the University of British Columbia in Vancouver, Canada and I spent three years as a Spitzer postdoctoral fellow at the National Optical Astronomy Observatory in Tucson, Arizona.</p>


        <p>My research is focussed on: galaxy formation and evolution, submillimetre and infrared galaxies, star formation rates, active galactic nuclei, spectral energy distributions, interstellar medium conditions at high redshift, galaxy clusters, and the cosmic infrared background.</p>

    </section>
